## Fixes

- The companion's self-update now works. In 0.8.0 and 0.8.1 it downloaded the update and then quietly threw it away, so nothing happened and you were asked again on every start.

## New

- Release notes are now built into the companion. After an update it shows what changed, and you can reopen them any time from Help > Release notes.

## Notes

- Because 0.8.0 and 0.8.1 contain the update bug, they cannot update themselves. Download this release from the releases page and replace your installed companion once. Updates after this one will install through the companion.
