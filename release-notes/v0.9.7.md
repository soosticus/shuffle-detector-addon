## A cleaner look and a professions-window tab

- Every ShuffleDetector window has a new flat theme: dark panels with clean edges and clear, narrow text, in the client's warm gold-on-brown colors so it blends with the professions window. Labels are shorter and the browser's filters are proper dropdowns that mark the current choice.
- The recipe browser is now a side tab on the Professions window (the gold coin under your profession tabs). Select it to browse and plan shuffles without leaving the window; your profession tabs or closing the window bring the normal crafting page back. `/shuffledetector browse` toggles that tab while the window is open, and a standalone window otherwise.
- The shuffle window attaches to the right of whatever you have open — professions, vendor, auction house, mail or bank — like the client's own side-by-side windows, and sits clear of the professions window's side tabs. With nothing open it is freely draggable as before and remembers its position.

## What you need to do

- Install the **ForeverCore** addon: it is now required, alongside Auctionator. It is the shared framework that provides professions-window tabs (SkillUp Forever users already have it). Without it the addon will not load.
- Install the update and `/reload`.

## Caveats

- Disenchanting is still not valued; in-game disenchant yields remain planned for v0.10.0.
- The new theme, tab and window attachment are freshly reworked: if something does not fit your UI scale, or the shuffle window misses a window it should attach to, please report it.
