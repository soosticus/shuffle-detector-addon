## Craft, shop and track a batch from one window

- Set a finished-item target and material price limits in game. The shuffle macro can buy eligible vendor materials, search the auction house, and buy within your limits. Every purchase needs a button press; commodity purchases need a separate quote-confirmation press.
- For supported learned recipes, buy raw cloth or bolts according to the cheaper live offer. Set only the raw-material limit: the bolt/bar limit is calculated from the conversion quantity.
- The same macro prepares stackable intermediates in a crafting queue, then crafts finished items in batches limited by materials, your target and bag space, leaving two general-purpose slots empty. Sell or disenchant the completed batch before crafting again.
- Batch controls and shuffle status share one compact window. Toggle it with `/shuffledetector shuffle`; rarely used controls are under **More controls**.
- Batch statistics show confirmed spending, income and averages, with estimated profit shown separately. Open **Analytics**, `/shuffledetector stats`, or the AddOn Settings entry for saved history and cash-flow charts. Verified untracked expenses and AH proceeds can be entered manually, including for older batches.

## Companion

- When running from a source checkout, disk changes refresh the companion without restarting its process. Lua/data edits retain the existing UI; Python edits preserve the native outer window and its screen/desktop placement. Calculator inputs and selection are retained.
- Packaged companions still use the normal self-updater.

## Getting started and caveats

- Update the addon and companion, resend your selected shuffle to include output quantities and estimated prices, then `/reload` in game. Open the character's profession window to record learned conversion recipes.
- Save a small target and inexpensive material limits first. Use `/click ShuffleDetectorShuffleButton` for shopping and crafting. Collect AH purchases from mail; unresolved purchases must be reconciled before retrying.
- Unsold disenchant loot and AH items are not actual income. Estimates use prices frozen at batch start; cash-flow charts do not prove an income improvement caused by the addon. History starts with this version and retains up to 200 batches per character.
- Two reserved slots provide loot headroom, not a guarantee that every disenchant result fits. Multi-step, shared-raw and multi-output intermediate conversions are not expanded.
- Shopping, queued crafts, accounting and the new layouts have automated test coverage, but all real-client edge cases are not yet confirmed. Avoid other AH searches while the snatcher is active.
- A source companion already running an older version needs one initial relaunch to acquire the new refresh/window behavior. Source refresh does not install the addon into WoW.
