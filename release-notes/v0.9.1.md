## Getting started

- Update the companion and addon, resend your selected shuffle, then `/reload` in game.
- Open your profession window to record raw-material conversion recipes. Start with a small batch and inexpensive price limits.
- Purchases remain click-confirmed. Unsold loot is not cash income; batch estimates are separate from confirmed transactions.
- Source companions need an initial relaunch to acquire the refreshed window behavior. Packaged builds retain the normal updater.
