## In-game recipe browser and batch calculator

- `/shuffledetector browse` ranks your recorded recipes by estimated profit from Auctionator's prices, without the companion app. Filter by profession, recipe status and exit (Vendor, AH), search by name, and select a row for reagent costs, exit values and why anything is not priced.
- The batch calculator plans a batch by craft count or budget, lists what to buy, and lets you enter your own cost for any material. If you have saved price limits, it also shows the batch at your limits: the most the materials can cost, the returns and the least profit.
- **Set active shuffle** makes a fully priced learned recipe your shuffle directly in game; the choice survives `/reload`. Browser filters and settings are remembered per character.
- **Unlearned patterns** now include recipes your other characters know, and **Professions to learn** lists recipes of professions this character does not have. The first time the browser opens (and after a game patch) it reads recipes of professions none of your characters has from the game, which takes a few seconds; `/shuffledetector catalog` rescans by hand.

## Shuffle button and shopping

- Crafting from materials already in your bags comes before shopping for the rest of the batch.
- Starting a craft at the AH also starts AH polling, and pressing the macro while the craft casts gets a quote, confirms it or buys an offer without interrupting the craft.
- While polling, the tracker shows what the last search found for each material (cheapest price against your limit, none listed, not enough gold, your own listing in the way, AH busy).
- AH and vendor offers shrink to what you can afford instead of disappearing when the whole batch costs more than your gold.
- Vendor reagents: one press buys up to five stacks (split into one-stack purchases), so bags keep room for AH materials; the next vendor visit tops them up.
- One press at a vendor sells every crafted piece in your bags.
- A vendor purchase that fails (for example "Inventory is full") no longer blocks the button: it settles itself from your bags.
- The shuffle window's estimate covers the whole batch target at your price limits, not just the crafts done so far.

## Getting started

- Install the update and `/reload`. Auctionator is still required for prices; scan with it before browsing.
- Disenchanting is not valued yet: there is no verified disenchant table for this client, so Disenchant exits stay unpriced. Disenchant yields and a cleanup of the companion app are planned for v0.10.0, a release candidate for 1.0.
- Not everything here has been confirmed in game yet, notably the professions-to-learn scan and buying during a craft. If something misbehaves, `/shuffledetector log` shows what happened.
