## The companion app is gone

ShuffleDetector is now just the addon. The desktop companion app (and its Windows, macOS and Flatpak downloads) is no longer released or supported; everything you used it for is in game:

- `/shuffledetector browse` ranks shuffles and plans batches.
- **Set active shuffle** in the browser replaces "Send selected shuffle to game". No `/reload` is needed.
- After login, the addon now looks up the names and vendor sell prices of every item in your recorded recipes by itself, so you no longer need the companion's "send items". `/shuffledetector items` repeats it.

## What you need to do

- Install the update and `/reload`. Auctionator is still required.
- If no shuffle is active after updating (one sent from the companion is no longer loaded), open `/shuffledetector browse`, pick the recipe and press **Set active shuffle**. Your price limits and batch progress are kept.
- You can delete the companion app. Leftover `Plan.lua` and `ItemList.lua` files in the addon folder are ignored and can be deleted too.

## Caveats

- Disenchanting is still not valued: the companion read Auctionator's disenchant table from disk, which the addon cannot do on this client. In-game disenchant yields are planned for v0.10.0, a release candidate for 1.0.
